Keep the hazmat site funded.

Tabocalypse has no servers to pay for, so every dollar goes to the humans building widgets, humor packs, and themes. Pick whichever platform you already use — a donation is a gift, not a subscription to anything.

Donations of any kind

One-time or recurring, whatever suits you. No perks, no members-only drops, no schedule. Nothing is promised in return beyond a thank-you. If a pack or widget ships later, everyone gets it on the same terms. Cancel a recurring tip any time.

GitHub Sponsors

Best if you already live on GitHub. One-time or monthly. Zero platform fee, so the most reaches the project.

Ko-fi

Simplest checkout, no account needed for a one-time tip. Recurring tips are available too.

Patreon

If Patreon is where you already support creators. A single recurring-tip tier with no perk text beyond keeping the project going.

How paid content works, if it ships

Nothing is scheduled or promised. If a one-time Tabocalypse Pro unlock, premium packs, or theme suites are ever sold, they are bought on a third-party merchant site and delivered as a signed file you paste or import. The extension verifies the signature offline. Nothing about your purchase is ever sent to us, because there is nowhere to send it.

All links open third-party sites governed by their own terms and privacy policies. Tabocalypse itself never processes payments. Details: PRIVACY.md · monetization plan.
